How I work through a problem

Every case study on this site follows the same four beats. It's the habit I'm trying to build: analysis is only finished when it changes what someone does.

  1. 01

    Frame the real question

    The stated problem is rarely the actual problem. Before touching data I write down what decision this analysis is supposed to inform, and what answer would change it.

  2. 02

    Interrogate the data

    Clean it, then try to break my own hypothesis. Cognitive science makes me suspicious of my first read — confirmation bias shows up fastest in your own charts.

  3. 03

    Find the mechanism

    A correlation isn't an insight. I push until I can name a plausible reason people behave the way the numbers say they do, and state what would disprove it.

  4. 04

    Make the call

    One recommendation, the tradeoff it carries, and how you'd know within a quarter whether it worked. Confidence stated honestly, not performed.
